YOUR INVENTORY WEBSITE

This folder is a complete website. It needs no database and no plugins.

WHAT IS HERE
  index.html          the page itself — ALWAYS replace your old copy with this one
  assets/             photos and documents this build actually packed

THIS IS YOUR FIRST BUILD
  Everything is included. Unzip this somewhere permanent on your computer
  (not a temp folder) — you'll reuse this same folder for every future update.

TO PUT IT ONLINE
  cPanel/FTP hosting: upload index.html and the assets folder into a folder
    such as public_html/inventory — your page is then at yoursite.com/inventory/
  Netlify: drag this whole folder onto Netlify's manual deploy area.

KEEP THE FOLDER STRUCTURE. index.html looks for assets/ files next to itself.
